# BioGPT: generative pre-trained transformer for biomedical text generation and mining

**Paper ID:** 11
**Authors:** Renqian Luo, Liai Sun, Yingce Xia, Tao Qin, Sheng Zhang, Hoifung Poon, Tie-Yan Liu
**Year:** 2022
**Venue:** Briefings in Bioinformatics, 2022
**DOI:** 10.1093/bib/bbac409
**Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36156661/

## Abstract

Pre-trained language models have attracted increasing attention in the biomedical domain, inspired by their success in the general natural language domain. Among the two main branches of pre-trained language models in the general language domain, BERT and GPT variants, the first has been extensively studied in the biomedical domain, such as BioBERT and PubMedBERT. While they have achieved great success on a variety of discriminative downstream biomedical tasks, their lack of generation ability constrains their application scope. In this paper, we propose BioGPT, a domain-specific generative Transformer language model pre-trained on large-scale biomedical literature. We evaluate BioGPT on six biomedical NLP tasks and demonstrate that our model outperforms previous models on most tasks, with particularly strong results on biomedical relation extraction and PubMedQA.
