# PMC-LLaMA: toward building open-source language models for medicine

**Paper ID:** 13
**Authors:** Chaoyi Wu et al.
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association, 31(9), 1833–1843
**DOI:** 10.1093/jamia/ocae045
**Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11639126/

## Abstract

Recently, large language models have showcased capabilities in natural language understanding. While demonstrating proficiency in everyday conversations and question-answering situations, these models frequently struggle in domains that require precision, such as medical applications, due to their lack of domain-specific knowledge. In this article, we describe the procedure for building an open-source language model specifically designed for medicine applications, termed PMC-LLaMA. We adapt a general-purpose LLM toward the medical domain, involving data-centric knowledge injection through the integration of 4.8 million biomedical academic papers and 30,000 medical textbooks, as well as comprehensive domain-specific instruction fine-tuning encompassing medical question answering, reasoning and conversational dialogues. The resulting 13B-parameter model is evaluated on public medical QA benchmarks and manual ratings.
