# KEBLM: Knowledge-Enhanced Biomedical Language Models

**Paper ID:** 10
**Authors:** Tuan Manh Lai, ChengXiang Zhai, Heng Ji
**Year:** 2023
**Venue:** Journal of Biomedical Informatics, 143, 104392
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.jbi.2023.104392
**Source:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1532046423001132

## Abstract

Pretrained language models have demonstrated strong performance on many natural language processing tasks. Despite their great success, these pretrained language models are typically pretrained only on unstructured free texts without leveraging existing structured knowledge bases that are readily available for many domains, especially scientific domains. As a result, these models may not achieve satisfactory performance on knowledge-intensive tasks such as biomedical NLP. Comprehending a complex biomedical document without domain-specific knowledge is challenging, even for humans. After the consolidation phase, the complete knowledge-enhanced model can be fine-tuned for any downstream task of interest to achieve optimal performance. Extensive experiments on many biomedical NLP datasets show that the proposed framework consistently improves the performance of the underlying pretrained language models on various downstream tasks such as natural language inference, question answering, and entity linking.
