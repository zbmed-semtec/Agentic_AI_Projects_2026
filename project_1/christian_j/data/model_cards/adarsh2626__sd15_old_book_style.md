##Description
This model is a Stable Diffusion 1.5–based fine-tune specialized in generating old art style illustrations inspired by vintage printed artwork from the late 19th and early 20th centuries. It produces images with engraved linework, muted antique color palettes, visible paper texture, and storybook-style compositions reminiscent of classic illustrations, ex-libris plates, and early lithographs. The style is baked into the model, allowing it to render scenes, characters, and objects in a consistent historical illustration aesthetic.


## Usage

You can use this model to generate old art style images just by a prompt.

## System

You can run this on Kaggle or Google collab or your own PC with at least 4 GB of VRAM

## Implementation requirements

It was trained using P100 GPU of Kaggle.

Its a fine-tuned model with lora adapter on Old Art Style Images with Captions Dataset
